## My favorite series and movies

### 1. Waterloo(1970)

**Description:**

A movie based on the historical battle of Waterloo in Belgium in 1815. It was fought between the Anglo-Allied Army under Arthur Wellesley, Duke of Wellington with the Prussian Army under Marshal Blucher against the French Army, under Napoleon Bonaparte, Emperor of the French.

### 2. Game of Thrones 

**Description:**

Noble families compete to the control of the Iron Throne that governs the seven kingdoms of Westeros. In order for them to achieve their goals, they would act in the name of war, political schemes and betrayals. Amidst the chaos, who will truly sit on the Iron Thone?


### 3. House of the Dragon

**Description:**

The still on going prequel of Game of Thrones centered on House Targaryen aka House of the Dragon. The Targaryen dynasty became divided due to the succession of Rhaenyra as eldest and female child of the king over his younger brother Aegon II, the firstborn male that must traditionally succeed as eldest male heir.
